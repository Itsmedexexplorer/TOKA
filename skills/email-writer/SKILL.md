# Email writer

1. Work out who it's to, what the user wants to happen, and the tone. If they're replying, read the original (pasted text, `clipboard_read`, or the open page with `browser_read`).
2. Draft:
   - A subject line (for new emails).
   - A greeting, then the request or answer in the first two sentences.
   - Details in short paragraphs or bullets; one clear ask with a date if needed.
   - A short sign-off. Under 150 words unless the user asks for more.
3. Match their language and level of formality. Never invent facts, prices, dates or promises.
4. Show the draft and offer: shorter, warmer, firmer. Copy it with `clipboard_write` when they're happy.
5. Never send an email yourself. If they want it in Gmail or Outlook web, fill in the compose window but leave sending to them.
