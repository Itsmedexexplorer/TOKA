# Daily planner

1. Collect today's tasks (typed, pasted, or from TO'KA's notes) and fixed commitments with times. Ask what time they start and finish if it isn't clear.
2. Estimate each task honestly and mark the top three that matter most.
3. Plan the day:
   - Hardest or most important work in the first focused block.
   - Blocks of 25–90 minutes with 5–15-minute breaks; batch small tasks together.
   - Leave about 20% of the day free for surprises.
   - Move what doesn't fit to "Tomorrow" rather than overpacking.
4. Show it as a simple time table. Offer to save it (`create_docx`) and to set reminders for the key blocks with `reminder_set`.
