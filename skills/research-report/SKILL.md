# Research report

1. Restate the question in one line and pick 3–5 angles to cover.
2. For each angle, `web_search`, then open 2–3 of the best results with `browser_open`. Prefer primary sources (official sites, papers, documentation) over listicles, and note each source's date.
3. Write:
   - **Answer in brief** (3–5 sentences).
   - **Findings** by angle, each claim with its source in brackets.
   - A **comparison table** when comparing options.
   - **Caveats**: conflicting sources, outdated information, what's unknown.
   - **Sources**: numbered, with title, site and date.
4. Save with `create_pdf` (or `create_docx` if they want to edit) and give a three-line summary in chat.
