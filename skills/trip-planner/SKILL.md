# Trip planner

1. Confirm the essentials, asking only for what's missing: destination, dates or number of days, budget level, who's travelling, and interests.
2. Research with `web_search` and `browser_open`: the top sights, opening days and hours, how long to spend at each, and the best area to stay. Prefer official sites.
3. Build a day-by-day plan that groups nearby places, with morning, afternoon and evening, travel time between stops, and one food suggestion per day. Leave slack; don't cram.
4. Add a budget table (stay, food, transport, tickets) as ranges in the local currency, and a short packing and practical-tips list (weather for those dates, local transport card, tipping).
5. Save with `create_pdf` as `Trip – <destination>.pdf`. Mention that prices and hours change and should be double-checked before booking. Never book or pay for anything.
